The Story Behind propAIty

It started with a simple frustration: watching talented real estate professionals waste hours on tools that didn't talk to each other.

The "Aha" Moment

Our founder, Alex Johnson, spent 6 months helping a friend sell their property. The experience was eye-opening: 7 different platforms, 4 spreadsheets, countless emails, and still—mismatched buyers, missed opportunities, and endless inefficiency.

"There has to be a better way. What if AI could understand what buyers actually want, not just what they click on?"

That question became our mission. In 2023, we assembled a team of real estate veterans and AI specialists to build the platform we wished existed: one that understands human intent, matches with precision, and eliminates friction completely.
